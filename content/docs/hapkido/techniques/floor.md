## Floor Techniques

These techniques are done from the floor; You are on your back, presumably after a throw or fall.
Knees are up.

1.  Attacker tries to stomp your face.  Plum blossom block pushing attacker's foot to the side (not across).
Grab with both hands above knee and pull backward. Back fist to groin, then right axe kick, left leg clear,
right chin strike + axe kick.

2.  Same attack as above.  Plum blossom to push foot across your body. 
Double punch to groin. Double grab above knee, pull down. Back fist to groin. Grab
foot or toes and twist foot to force attacker to turn over. Finish
with sweeping kick to the head.

3.  Same attack again.  Plum blossom to push foot to the side, grab above knee to 
pull down. This time you end up further away than before. Shrimp in with knee strike to coccyx.

4.  Attacker is punching downward towards your face.  You grab the
wrist as it is coming down and pull the attacker off balance so that he
rolls over your body. Roll towards attacker with punch to the face, continuing into an arm 
bar.

5.  Attacker grabs you by both ankles and pulls you forward.
Close in with the attacker using your feet to apply 
leg locks. Off balance, then punch.
